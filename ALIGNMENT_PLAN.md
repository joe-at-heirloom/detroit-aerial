# Alignment execution plan

Prepared 2026-09-05. Scope: all layers currently listed in the viewer manifest, including downtown, historical west blocks, downloaded modern orthophotos, and the displayed Today imagery. This document plans the work; it does not change imagery or saved alignments.

Objective: make unchanged ground features occupy the same geographic position across years, while preserving continuity between photographs. Treat placement, camera/lens geometry, scan deformation, and relief displacement as separate possible error sources. Lens distortion is a hypothesis to test, not an established explanation for every mismatch.

1. **Capture a reproducible baseline.** Inventory the exact manifest builds, source frames, COLMAP models, preprocessing, camera parameters, raster grids, and saved hand adjustments. Check that source files and reconstruction outputs are available before scheduling rebuilds. Record versions and checksums; use separate candidate output directories. Freeze the modern reference imagery, including the Today comparison sample, so online imagery changes cannot move the benchmark. Measure downtown 1949 with and without its saved hand adjustment, preserving the original. Measured 2026-09-23 with `dtcross.py` (1.25 m/px, 5x5): unadjusted, 1949 vs 1961 is 3.2 m median (p90 8.7) and 1949 vs 1956 is 4.0 m; the committed adjustment (+41.2 E, +2.2 N, x0.95) makes those 35.2 / 43.3 m, and the uncommitted working-tree one (+20.6 E, +17.0 N, x0.95) 44.0 / 38.7 m. Both worsen film-to-film agreement roughly tenfold, consistent with the roof-lean trap in HANDOFF; the committed `dist/` tiles bake the committed one in. The baseline should be the unadjusted layer. Audit crop, resize, and padding transforms: the current script normalizes some scans by image height, an assumption that needs checking against actual image content and scan batches.

   Deliverable: baseline inventory, reproducible build commands, coverage map, and baseline displacement report for the imagery actually served.

2. **Build one trustworthy measurement system.** Extend the pairwise approach in `scripts/dtcross.py` (it crashed on every run until 2026-09-23, from a shadowed variable; downtown pairs against Today are roof-dominated and are not ground evidence) to all overlapping manifest layers using windowed raster reads. Use `pipeline/handadjust.py` for saved adjustments. Measure coarse offsets before fine matching to catch whole-block street-grid aliases. Record displacement vectors, confidence, sample counts, rejected matches, and missing coverage. Calibrate the measurements with planted translations and known synthetic geometric distortions. Adapt within-frame and seam diagnostics to the COLMAP renderer; `characterise.py` and `seamcheck.py` currently use older placement/rendering paths and cannot automatically certify current COLMAP builds.

   Select spatially distributed stable ground landmarks, including distinctive intersections and railway features, and reserve geographic regions and entire overlap pairs for validation. Keep final acceptance checkpoints out of every fit and model-selection step. Exclude roofs, water, vegetation, shadows, and visibly rebuilt ground from control where feasible. Low-confidence or unmatched areas remain explicitly unverified.

   Deliverable: all-year pairwise error matrix, spatial error maps, and fixed validation datasets.

3. **Diagnose photographic distortion on a pilot.** Start with a connected subset of 1961 spanning adjacent flight lines, frame centres, corners, and edges; then test a difficult 1949 subset containing different scan batches. Plot residual vectors both in map coordinates and relative to each photograph's centre. Constant displacement suggests placement; patterns repeating with image radius suggest lens/calibration issues; directional or batch-specific patterns suggest scan geometry; errors tied to tall objects suggest relief. These signatures guide experiments but do not uniquely identify a cause. Compare neighbouring exposures of the same ground to reduce confusion from historical change.

   Deliverable: evidence identifying which error components a whole-map adjustment cannot remove, and whether raw-frame recalibration is justified.

4. **Test camera and scan corrections before full rebuilds.** Preserve the current fixed `SIMPLE_RADIAL` solve as the control. Compare bounded calibration variants, first the existing one-term model, then `RADIAL`, and only if warranted a model with tangential distortion such as `OPENCV`. Group shared calibration by supported camera/flight/scan evidence; do not assume all years share one lens or give every frame unlimited freedom. Keep focal length and camera height constrained because this project's flat terrain has already produced degenerate self-calibration. Audit principal-point placement after cropping and padding. `colmap_render.py` projects `SIMPLE_RADIAL`, `RADIAL` and `PINHOLE`; add and verify `OPENCV` projection before using it. These camera experiments apply to the west blocks only: downtown mosaics come from the older pipeline plus `fixdowntown.py`, not COLMAP, and need their own path.

   Distinguish the fitted quadratic reconstruction surface from physical terrain. Test a suitable ground elevation model if available, with consistent horizontal and vertical coordinates, without introducing modern buildings into historical geometry. Apply scan-specific corrections only when repeatable evidence supports them. Fit geometry jointly through overlap ties; do not independently pull each frame toward modern imagery.

   Acceptance: improvement on withheld overlaps and ground checkpoints beyond measurement uncertainty, stable parameters across subsets, no loss of connected frames or usable coverage, and no material seam regression. If a more complex model fails, retain the simpler model and document the unresolved distortion.

5. **Establish the shared geographic reference and solve across years.** Provisionally select downloaded 2016 NAIP based on the recorded comparisons (1.5 m median, but measured against Esri, so its rank is agreement with Esri), checking it against 2022 and other modern orthophotos first. Agreement with Esri alone does not establish absolute truth. Hold one reference fixed; reserve another source for independent evaluation. Build confidence-weighted correspondences from each year to that reference and between years where unchanged ground is identifiable. Solve the layer corrections together with robust outlier rejection so historical years can help one another without a chain of inherited offsets.

   Start with translation, rotation, and uniform scale. Add affine or smooth low-order corrections only when spatially withheld validation improves. Constrain smooth warps against excessive local scale changes or foldovers and avoid unconstrained extrapolation beyond control coverage. Preserve historical seam geometry as an acceptance condition. Correct the documented 2018 offset only after confirming its spatial boundary and source-tile behaviour; do not shift its entire layer to fix one region. Validate downtown separately and include downtown-to-west comparisons wherever coverage overlaps.

   Deliverable: versioned correction models, control provenance, uncertainty maps, and candidate rasters. Compose transformations and minimize repeated resampling when producing final imagery.

6. **Validate at full extent before adopting candidates.** Compare baseline and candidate on the same independent checkpoints, every measurable overlapping year pair, within-year photo seams, and an independent modern source. Report median, p90, worst reliable residual, and coverage at multiple spatial scales, including frame edges and block boundaries. Inspect blink/wipe comparisons at native zoom in downtown, ordinary neighbourhoods, flight-line seams, the 1956 outer edges, and the affected 2018 region.

   Proposed ground-alignment targets: median at or below 3 m; p90 at or below 10 m; investigate every reliable residual above 25 m. Preserve or improve baseline seam statistics within measurement uncertainty. These are acceptance targets, not promised accuracy; unsupported regions and persistent outliers must be listed. Pixel-perfect agreement at 0.63 m resolution is a separate, stricter target. Rooftop movement is evaluated separately and may require dense historical 3D reconstruction beyond this pass.

7. **Integrate reproducibly and retain rollback.** Rebuild only candidates that pass the pilot and validation gates. Update the local manifest to explicit accepted build names, reconcile saved hand adjustments so they are applied exactly once, and verify that measurement, local tiles, and static tiles agree. Rebuild affected static tiles with explicit invalidation of stale tiles; the existing static builder normally skips files already present. Retain previous builds and manifest/adjustment snapshots. Update README accuracy claims from the new report and remove superseded conflicting figures. Hosting or publishing is a separate action from this alignment plan.

   Deliverable: aligned local viewer, updated static artifacts if required, before/after report, reproducible commands, and rollback instructions.

Execution order: baseline and measurement system → distortion pilot → accepted photographic corrections → modern-reference checks and joint placement → full validation → integration. Time the pilot before estimating full reconstruction and raster-generation costs. Do not rebuild every year until the pilot demonstrates a measurable benefit.

Technical references: [COLMAP camera models](https://colmap.github.io/cameras.html) explains the radial and tangential model families; [COLMAP calibration guidance](https://colmap.github.io/faq.html) covers shared and fixed camera parameters. Project-specific findings above come from the checked-in scripts, manifest, adjustment file, README, and HANDOFF; historical accuracy claims must be remeasured against the current builds.
